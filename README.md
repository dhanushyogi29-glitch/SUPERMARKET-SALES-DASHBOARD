📘 Supermarket Sales Dashboard – Power BI Project

🧩 Overview :
This project focuses on analyzing supermarket sales performance to uncover insights about product categories, sales trends, profit margins, and customer purchasing behavior. An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to help management make data-driven decisions that boost sales and profitability.

🎯 Objectives :

Track total sales, profit, and quantity sold over time.

Analyze product category performance.

Identify top-selling products and regions.

Monitor monthly and yearly growth trends.

Support management in making strategic decisions for pricing and inventory.

📁 Dataset Details :
File Name: Supermarket_Sales_Data.xlsx
Total Records: 5,000+ Transactions

Key Columns:
Invoice ID | Date | Product Category | Product Name | Quantity | Unit Price | Total Sales | Profit | Branch | City | Payment Method | Customer Type

🛠 Tools & Technologies :

Microsoft Power BI – Dashboard creation & data visualization

Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning & preprocessing

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – For calculated measures and KPIs

📈 Key Insights :

Top Performing Category: Beverages and Snacks contribute the highest sales.

Monthly Trend: Sales peak in December, indicating festive demand.

Profit Analysis: Average profit margin is 18% across all categories.

Branch Performance: Branch A recorded the highest total revenue.

Payment Insights: 45% of customers prefer digital payments.

Customer Behavior: Regular customers contribute 70% of total revenue.

🚀 Business Impact :

Improved visibility into store and product performance.

Helped identify high-demand and low-performing products.

Enabled data-driven discount and marketing strategies.

Supported better inventory and supply chain planning.
